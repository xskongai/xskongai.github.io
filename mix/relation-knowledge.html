Relation extraction · taxonomy induction · 1992 → 2026

Relation Knowledge from Text

From patterns to large language models

从文本获取关系知识：从模板到大模型

Text states facts like “a Bambara ndang is a kind of bow lute” all the time. Turning such statements into relation knowledge, triples of the form (x, relation, y) that make up taxonomies and knowledge graphs, is one of the oldest tasks in NLP. Four generations of methods have tackled it. Lexico-syntactic patterns, starting with Hearst (1992), were the first.

文本里时时刻刻在陈述“Bambara ndang 是一种弓形琉特琴”这样的事实。把这些陈述变成关系知识，也就是构成分类体系和知识图谱的 (x, 关系, y) 三元组，是 NLP 最古老的任务之一。先后有四代方法来解决它，从 Hearst (1992) 开始的词汇-句法模板是第一代。

The task in one line · 一句话说明任务

The bow lute, such as the Bambara ndang, is plucked …

sentence→ Bambara ndangis-abow lute →one edge of a taxonomy
hypernym (category) 上位词 trigger 触发结构 hyponym (member) 下位词

A first-generation method reads the relation off the highlighted structure. Later generations learn it from data or ask a language model. Example from Hearst (1992).

第一代方法直接从高亮的结构中读出关系；后几代方法从数据中学习，或者去问语言模型。例子出自 Hearst (1992)。

Main line of this page · 本页主线

Every generation trades precision against coverage, and the same failures (sparsity, drift, paraphrase, ambiguity) return in each one in a new form. Patterns never disappeared: later generations absorbed them as features, prompts and rules.

每一代方法都在精度与覆盖之间取舍，稀疏、漂移、改写、歧义这些问题在每一代都以新的形式重现。模板也从未消失，后来的方法把它吸收成了特征、prompt 和规则。

SECTION 1

The task 任务是什么

Relation knowledge is a set of facts of the form (x, relation, y): is-a links that form a taxonomy, part-whole links, attributes, causal links, or any relation in a knowledge graph. The task is to obtain these facts from text, at scale, with as little hand-labeled data as possible.

关系知识是一组 (x, 关系, y) 形式的事实：构成分类体系的上下位链接、部分-整体链接、属性、因果，或知识图谱中的任意关系。任务是从文本中大规模地获取这些事实，并尽量少用人工标注数据。

SECTION 3 · GEN 1

The pattern idea 模板思想

A lexico-syntactic pattern is a fixed piece of syntax that, when it sits between two noun phrases, signals a semantic relation between them. Matching such patterns against raw text turns sentences into relation triples with no labeled training data.

词汇-句法模板是一段固定的句法结构。当它出现在两个名词短语之间时，就指示两者之间的某种语义关系。用模板去匹配原始文本，不需要任何标注数据就能把句子变成关系三元组。

Hearst's three criteria for a good pattern Hearst 提出的三条标准

Criterion 1

Frequent

Occurs often, and in many genres of text.

在多种文体中频繁出现。

Criterion 2

Reliable

Almost always indicates the relation of interest.

几乎总是指示目标关系。

Criterion 3

Knowledge-free

Recognizable without prior knowledge of the words involved.

不需要预先懂得相关词的含义就能识别。

Hearst also sketched how to find new patterns: take word pairs already known to stand in the relation, collect the contexts where they co-occur, and generalize what those contexts share. That procedure is the seed of bootstrapping (Section 6).

Hearst 还给出了发现新模板的流程：拿已知具有该关系的词对，收集它们在语料中共现的上下文，归纳这些上下文的共同点。这个流程就是后来 bootstrapping 的雏形（见第 6 节）。

Structural pattern vs lexical trigger 结构型模板与词汇型触发词

Structural · one trigger, every domain

medicine
NSAIDs such as ibuprofen

law
felonies such as burglary

sport
sports such as cricket

The words such as carry no meaning of “category”. The structure does the work, so the pattern transfers to any domain.

such as 这几个词本身没有“类别”的意思，起作用的是结构，所以它能迁移到任何领域。

The approach scales. WebIsADb (Seitner et al., 2016) ran 59 patterns gathered from the literature over a web-scale crawl and extracted more than 400 million is-a pairs. Probase (Wu et al., 2012) used Hearst patterns on web text to build a taxonomy of millions of concepts.

SECTION 6 · GEN 1

Learning patterns automatically 自动学习模板

Hand-written patterns are precise but few. From 1998 on, systems grew patterns from a handful of seeds and scored them, and Open IE later dropped the fixed list of relations altogether.

手写模板准确但数量少。从 1998 年起，系统开始从少量种子出发自动扩展模板并给模板打分；之后 Open IE 干脆不再预设关系列表。

6.1 Bootstrapping from seeds, 1998– 从种子出发的 bootstrapping

Instead of writing patterns, start from a few seed pairs and let pairs and patterns find each other in alternation.

不再手写模板，而是从少量种子词对出发，让词对和模板交替地互相发现。

Pattern reliability in Espresso Espresso 的模板可靠度

rπ(p) = (1 / |I|) · Σi∈I [ pmi(i, p) / maxpmi ] · rι(i)

A pattern is reliable when it has high PMI with reliable instances. Instance reliability is defined the same way in reverse, so the two scores are updated in alternation.

一条模板如果与可靠的实例有高 PMI，它就可靠。实例的可靠度用对称的方式定义，所以两种分数交替更新。

Y such as X · target: is-a

  • ✓ (fruit, apple)
  • ✓ (sport, cricket)
  • ✓ (country, France)
  • ✗ (problem, this)

  • ✓ (car, engine)
  • ✗ (John, a cold)
  • ✗ (company, 500 employees)
  • ✗ (she, a meeting)

Illustrative matches. The first pattern binds mostly to on-target pairs; the second binds to almost anything, so its reliability score is low. · 示意性的匹配结果。前一条模板主要命中目标词对，后一条几乎什么都命中，所以可靠度低。

Semantic drift, and how anchoring stops it 语义漂移与双锚定

Fix: doubly-anchored pattern. cities such as Paris and * admits Rome, Madrid and Berlin, but not the balcony, because the class word cities and a trusted member Paris must both be present (Kozareva, Riloff & Hovy 2008).

对策：双锚定模板。“cities such as Paris and *”能引入 Rome、Madrid、Berlin，但引入不了 the balcony，因为类名 cities 和可信成员 Paris 必须同时出现。

6.3 Open information extraction, 2007– 开放信息抽取

TextRunner (Banko et al., 2007) extracted triples with no fixed list of relations. ReVerb (Fader et al., 2011) showed that one syntactic constraint on the relation phrase removes most incoherent and uninformative extractions. After a neural phase, LLMs moved Open IE toward generative extraction from 2023 onward (Liu et al., 2024 survey).

TextRunner 不预设关系列表直接抽取三元组。ReVerb 证明了：对关系短语加一条句法约束，就能去掉大部分不连贯、没有信息量的抽取结果。经过神经网络阶段后，从 2023 年起，大模型把 Open IE 推向了生成式抽取。

Part II · Generation 2 · 2005–2020

Statistical and neural learning

Relations are learned from data: sentences labeled through a knowledge base, word embeddings, or annotated benchmarks. Patterns become features and a source of supervision. Section 7.

第二部分 · 第二代：统计与神经学习。关系从数据中学习：通过知识库自动标注的句子、词向量或人工标注的基准。模板变成了特征和监督信号的来源。第 7 节。

SECTION 7 · GEN 2

Learning relations from data 从数据中学习关系

7.1 Distant supervision 远程监督

Instead of writing patterns, align a knowledge base with text and let every co-occurrence of a known pair become a training example. Labels come for free, and so does noise.

不再编写模板，而是把知识库和文本对齐，让已知词对的每次共现都成为训练样本。标签不花钱，噪声也随之而来。

WorkStep forward

The first distant supervision: Snow et al. (2005) 最早的远程监督

WordNet pair (dog, animal)→ corpus sentences with both→ dependency path features→ hypernym classifier

Snow, Jurafsky & Ng (2005) labeled sentences with word pairs already linked in WordNet, represented each sentence by the dependency path between the two nouns, and trained a classifier on those paths. The learner recovered Hearst’s patterns and found new ones such as Y like X, Y called X and the appositive X, a Y. Labeling text with a knowledge base this way is distant supervision, years before Mintz et al. (2009) made it standard in relation extraction.

Snow 等人 (2005) 用 WordNet 中已有关系的词对去标注语料句子，把句子表示为两个名词之间的依存路径，再用这些路径训练分类器。学习器重新发现了 Hearst 的模板，还找到了 Y like X、Y called X 和同位语 X, a Y 等新模板。用知识库标注文本的做法就是远程监督，比 Mintz 等人 (2009) 把它变成关系抽取的标准做法早了好几年。

7.2 Distributional evidence for hypernymy 上下位的分布式证据

Patterns need two words in the same sentence. Distributional methods compare the contexts each word appears in across the whole corpus, so they cover pairs that never co-occur.

模板要求两个词出现在同一句话里。分布式方法比较的是每个词在整个语料中出现的上下文，所以能覆盖从不共现的词对。

Caution: lexical memorization. Levy et al. (2015) showed that supervised classifiers over word embeddings often learn whether y is a typical hypernym, such as animal or person, rather than how x and y relate.

注意“词汇记忆”问题：Levy 等人 (2015) 发现，基于词向量的有监督分类器常常学到的是 y 是不是一个典型的上位词（比如 animal、person），而不是 x 和 y 之间的关系。

7.3 Patterns plus distributional models, 2016–2020 模板与分布式表示结合

Pattern evidence is precise but extremely sparse: two words must co-occur in exactly the right configuration. HypeNET (Shwartz et al., 2016) encoded dependency paths with an LSTM and combined them with word embeddings. Roller, Kiela & Nickel (2018) showed that simple pattern-based models beat distributional ones on several hypernymy benchmarks, and that a low-rank embedding of the pattern matrix fixes much of the sparsity. Yu et al. (2020) used distributional models for pairs that patterns never cover.

模板证据准确但极其稀疏：两个词必须以恰好正确的结构共现。HypeNET 用 LSTM 编码依存路径并与词向量结合。Roller 等人 (2018) 证明简单的模板方法在多个上位词基准上胜过分布式方法，而对模板矩阵做低秩嵌入能解决大部分稀疏问题。Yu 等人 (2020) 对模板覆盖不到的词对用分布式模型补充。

score(x, y) = uxᵀ Σr vy

Illustrative counts. Rows are candidate hyponyms, columns candidate hypernyms. Roller et al. apply SVD to a PPMI matrix; this demo uses log(1 + count) so the arithmetic stays visible.

示意性计数。行是候选下位词，列是候选上位词。Roller 等人对 PPMI 矩阵做 SVD；本演示用 log(1 + 计数)，方便看清计算过程。

SECTION 8 · GEN 3

Pretrained language models 预训练语言模型

8.1 The direction flips: extraction pattern vs verbalization template 方向反转：抽取模板与关系说法模板

8.2 Fine-tuned encoders and generated triples 微调编码器与生成三元组

Matching the Blanks · 2019

[E1] Steve Jobs [/E1] founded [E2] Apple [/E2] in 1976.

Baldini Soares et al. mark the two entities and read the relation from the marker tokens of a BERT encoder. Extra pretraining teaches the model that sentences sharing an entity pair tend to share a relation.

在输入中标出两个实体，从 BERT 中实体标记位置的表示读出关系。额外的预训练让模型学到：共享同一实体对的句子往往表达同一关系。

REBEL · 2021

<triplet> Apple <subj> Steve Jobs <obj> founded by

Huguet Cabot & Navigli train a sequence-to-sequence model (BART) that writes the triples out as text, end to end. It points ahead to the generative extraction of Generation 4.

训练一个序列到序列模型（BART），端到端地把三元组直接写成文本。它预示了第四代的生成式抽取。

8.3 Patterns as prompts, 2019–2023 模板变成 prompt

The direction flips. Instead of mining text, a template is filled with a subject and a blank, and a language model predicts the blank. Templates become a way to measure what a model knows.

方向反转了。不再从文本里挖掘，而是把主语和空位填进模板，让语言模型预测空位。模板变成了测量模型知道什么的工具。

Probe · LAMA 2019

Paris is the capital of [MASK].

A knowledge-base triple is verbalized with a fixed template; the model fills the blank. · 用固定模板把知识库三元组写成句子，让模型填空。

Consistency · ParaRel 2021

Paris is the capital of [MASK].
[MASK]'s capital is Paris.
The capital city of [MASK] is Paris.

One fact, three wordings. ParaRel measures how often predictions agree across paraphrases like these; for the models tested, often they did not. · 同一事实三种问法，模型的回答经常不一致。

Anchored prompts · 2023
[MASK] such as keas.
→ fish ✗ A kea is a [MASK].
→ fish ✗ [MASK] such as keas and parrots.
→ birds ✓

Adding a known co-hyponym as an anchor helps, most of all for rare or abstract concepts. Example adapted from Liu, Cohn & Frermann (2023), Fig. 1. · 加入一个已知的共下位词作锚点更有效，对罕见或抽象概念尤其明显。

LPAQA (Jiang et al., 2020) and AutoPrompt (Shin et al., 2020) mined or optimized prompts automatically. Later work found that prompt-based results can be driven by bias in the prompt itself rather than by the model’s knowledge (Cao et al., 2021). Hypert (2023) continued pretraining BERT on sentences matched by Hearst patterns. Tikhomirov & Loukachevitch found that the prompts that work best for LLMs track the classic patterns, so prompts can be screened with small models first.

LPAQA 和 AutoPrompt 自动挖掘或优化 prompt。后来的研究发现，基于 prompt 的结果可能主要由 prompt 自身的偏置决定，而不是模型的知识。Hypert (2023) 用 Hearst 模板匹配到的句子对 BERT 做继续预训练。Tikhomirov 和 Loukachevitch 发现，对大模型最有效的 prompt 与经典模板高度一致，所以可以先用小模型筛选 prompt。

Part IV · Generation 4 · 2023–

Large language models

LLMs generate pairs, taxonomies and knowledge graphs directly, by prompting or fine-tuning, and write rules for other systems to run. Section 9.

第四部分 · 第四代：大模型。大模型通过 prompt 或微调直接生成词对、分类体系和知识图谱，还会编写规则交给其他系统执行。第 9 节。

SECTION 9 · GEN 4

LLMs and progress in 2024–2026 大模型与 2024–2026 最新进展

9.1 LLMs solve taxonomy tasks directly 大模型直接做分类体系任务

TaxoLLaMA ACL 2024 Semantic Web 2026

LLaMA-2-7B fine-tuned on WordNet hypernym pairs. Across hypernym discovery, taxonomy enrichment, taxonomy construction and lexical entailment it reached state of the art on 11 of 16 tasks and second place on 4. About 75% of its hypernym-discovery errors were hypernyms that were too general.

在 WordNet 上下位词对上微调的 LLaMA-2-7B。在四类任务的 16 个任务中拿下 11 项 SOTA、4 项第二。上位词发现中约 75% 的错误是给出的上位词过于宽泛。

arXiv 2403.09207 · journal version

Sister terms leak into training 2025

Sadkovskii, Loukachevitch & Grishin study the bias that co-hyponyms of test items, left in WordNet-based training data, introduce into hypernym-discovery scores, beyond the usual removal of the test items themselves.

研究训练数据中残留的测试项共下位词（兄弟节点）给上位词发现评分带来的偏差，这超出了只删除测试项本身的常规做法。

paper (PDF)

LLM prompts track the classic patterns 2024

Tikhomirov & Loukachevitch found a strong correlation between how well a prompt works for hypernym prediction and the classic Hearst patterns it is built from, so prompt selection can start on small models.

发现 prompt 在上位词预测上的效果与其所基于的经典模板高度相关，所以可以先在小模型上筛选 prompt。

arXiv 2401.04515

9.2 Generative taxonomy construction and expansion 生成式分类体系构建与扩展

Chain-of-Layer CIKM 2024

LLMs asked for a whole taxonomy at once insert unrelated entities. Chain-of-Layer builds it one layer at a time in a hierarchical numbering format and filters each layer with an ensemble ranker. It follows TaxonomyGPT (Chen et al., 2023), the first attempt to prompt LLMs for hierarchical relations.

一次性让大模型生成整个分类体系会混入无关实体。Chain-of-Layer 用层级编号格式逐层构建，每层用集成排序过滤。它的前身是 TaxonomyGPT，最早用 prompt 让大模型预测层级关系。

arXiv 2402.07386

CodeTaxo ACL Findings 2025

Represents the taxonomy as Python Entity objects and turns expansion into code completion, e.g. thermochemistry.add_parent(chemistry).

把分类体系表示成 Python Entity 对象，把扩展任务变成代码补全。

arXiv 2408.09070

TaxoAdapt and BoostTaxo 2025 2026

TaxoAdapt (Kargupta et al., 2025) adjusts taxonomy width and depth to the topic distribution of a scientific corpus. BoostTaxo (2026) lets a small LLM filter candidate parents, a large LLM rank them, and structural features calibrate the edges, all zero-shot.

TaxoAdapt 根据科学文献的主题分布调整分类体系的宽度和深度。BoostTaxo 用小模型筛选候选父节点、大模型排序打分，再用结构特征校准边，全程零样本。

BoostTaxo, arXiv 2605.12520

9.3 Can LLMs replace taxonomies? 大模型能否替代分类体系

TaxoGlimpse VLDB 2024

Eighteen LLMs on ten taxonomies, from shopping to biology. They are reliable on common domains and weak on specialized ones and on leaf-level entities; in most taxonomies accuracy falls from root to leaf.

18 个大模型、10 个分类体系（从购物到生物）。在通用领域可靠，在专业领域和叶子层实体上表现差；大多数分类体系中，准确率从根到叶逐层下降。

arXiv 2406.11131

Models vs humans on semantic relations 2024–25

Cao et al. compared six pretrained models with humans on several semantic relations and found a significant knowledge gap. LLM-Hype (VLDB 2025 workshop) offers a targeted framework for hypernym–hyponym evaluation.

Cao 等人比较六个预训练模型与人类在多种语义关系上的知识，发现差距明显。LLM-Hype 提供了专门评估上下位推理的框架。

arXiv 2412.01131 · LLM-Hype (PDF)

9.4 Ontology learning benchmark: LLMs4OL 本体学习评测

LLMs4OL 2025 ISWC 2025 · Nara

Hybrid pipelines that combined commercial LLMs with domain-tuned embeddings and fine-tuning gave the strongest overall results.

把商业大模型与领域调优嵌入、微调方法结合的混合流水线整体效果最好。

overview paper

LLMs4OL 2026 ISWC 2026 · Bari, 25–29 Oct

Three tasks: build an ontology end to end from raw text, extend an existing ontology, and induce a taxonomy for an unseen domain. One participating team reported that its LLM pipeline favored hierarchical relations and extracted no non-taxonomic relations at all.

三个任务：从原始文本端到端构建本体、扩展已有本体、为未见领域归纳分类体系。一个参赛团队报告其大模型流水线偏向层级关系，最终一条非分类关系都没抽出来。

challenge site · team report

9.5 From text to knowledge graphs 从文本到知识图谱

KGGen NeurIPS 2025

An LLM extracts (subject, predicate, object) triples, then iterative LLM-guided clustering merges synonymous entities and relations to reduce sparsity. On the MINE benchmark released with it, KGGen scored 18.3 points above GraphRAG and 36.2 above OpenIE.

大模型抽取三元组，再用迭代聚类合并同义实体和关系，缓解稀疏。在其配套的 MINE 基准上，比 GraphRAG 高 18.3 个百分点，比 OpenIE 高 36.2 个百分点。

arXiv 2502.09956

Surveys of generative IE 2024–25

Generative information extraction now covers entity, relation and event extraction, plus unified models for all of them.

生成式信息抽取已覆盖实体、关系、事件抽取，以及统一多任务的模型。

Zhang et al., COLING 2025 · Xu et al., 2024 · Open IE survey, 2024

9.6 Rules come back, written by LLMs 规则回归：由大模型来写

RuleChef 2026

LLMs write executable rules for classification, NER or relation extraction and repair them on held-out failures. Only the rules run at inference, so the system is fast, deterministic and inspectable.

大模型为分类、NER、关系抽取编写可执行规则，并根据验证集上的失败案例修补。推理时只运行规则，所以系统快速、确定、可检查。

arXiv 2607.01293

Rules that guide LLM relation extraction NAACL 2025 2025

Low-resource RE with labeling rules that pair a contextual pattern with a relation label, used to steer LLM reasoning and annotation. Guo et al. use reinforcement learning to reward outputs that name the key relation words, the lexical triggers of Section 3.

用“上下文模式 + 关系标签”形式的规则引导大模型推理和标注。Guo 等人用强化学习奖励点出关键关系词的输出，这些关系词就是第 3 节说的词汇型触发词。

NAACL 2025 · arXiv 2510.06198

9.7 Rethinking template-based probing 反思基于模板的探测

BELIEF and MyriadLAMA 2024–25

Many prompts per fact, so accuracy, consistency and overconfidence can be measured instead of one accuracy number.

每个事实配多个 prompt，测量准确率、一致性和过度自信，而不只是一个准确率。

arXiv 2406.12277

Zero-prompt estimation and fluent templates WSDM 2025 EMNLP 2025

ZP-LKE drops templates and shows the model several (x, y) examples so it infers the relation itself. Semenov & Sennrich show that translated mLAMA templates ignore the grammar of inserted entities, which leads such benchmarks to underestimate multilingual knowledge.

ZP-LKE 不用模板，直接给模型多个 (x, y) 示例让它自己推断关系。Semenov 和 Sennrich 指出 mLAMA 的翻译模板忽略了填入实体的语法，导致这类基准低估了模型的多语言知识。

ZP-LKE · arXiv 2510.15115

SECTION 10 · ACROSS GENERATIONS

Problems that outlast every generation 贯穿各代的核心问题

Each problem below is shown in its first-generation form, where it is easiest to see. Section 11 shows the form it takes with large language models.

下面每个问题都以第一代的形式展示，因为那里最容易看清。第 11 节给出它们在大模型时代的形式。

Precision vs recall 精度与召回

Failure
Specific patterns are precise but rare. Generic ones fire everywhere: X is a Y on Python is a good choice yields (Python, is-a, good choice).具体模板准但少；泛化模板到处命中，会从 Python is a good choice 抽出错误关系。

Response
Score pattern reliability (Espresso); add semantic constraints (Girju et al.).给模板打可靠度分；加语义约束。

Sparsity 稀疏

Failure
poodle may never appear in “animals such as poodles”, so no pattern ever links it to animal.poodle 可能从未出现在 animals such as poodles 里，没有任何模板能把它和 animal 连起来。

Response
Low-rank smoothing (Roller et al. 2018); combine with embeddings (HypeNET).低秩平滑；结合词向量。

Semantic drift 语义漂移

Failure
cities → a trip to X → the beach → relax on X → the balcony.城市 → “a trip to X” → 海滩 → “relax on X” → 阳台。

Response
Confidence filtering; mutual exclusion between classes; doubly-anchored patterns.置信度过滤；类别互斥；双锚定模板。

Paraphrase coverage 改写覆盖

Failure
One relation, many forms: bought, took over, snapped up, now owned by. A single pattern catches one.一个关系有许多说法，一条模板只抓得到一种。

Response
Bootstrapping; DIRT paraphrase rules; LLM paraphrasing today.bootstrapping；DIRT 改写规则；现在多用大模型改写。

Ambiguity 歧义

Failure
Y of X: the engine of the car is part-whole, a cup of tea is a measure, the city of Paris is apposition.同样是 of：部分-整体、计量、同位，形式相同含义不同。

Response
Semantic constraints on the arguments; aggregate evidence over many sentences.对论元加语义约束；在大量句子上聚合证据。

Noun-phrase boundaries 名词短语边界

Failure
most European countries, especially France: the hypernym should be European country, not most European countries or just country.上位词应是 European country，抽多了或抽少了都不对。

Response
Parse the sentence; keep the head noun plus the modifiers that matter.做句法分析，保留中心词和必要的修饰语。

Context 语境

Failure
Early naturalists grouped whales, dolphins and other fish together yields whale is-a fish. The sentence reports a belief, not a fact.这句话转述的是观点而非事实，却抽出了“鲸鱼是鱼”。

Response
Filter negation, hedging and reported belief; trust only pairs seen often.过滤否定、模糊表达和转述；只信高频词对。

Prompt sensitivity (LM era) prompt 敏感

Failure
Rewording a prompt for the same fact changes the model’s answer.同一事实换个问法，模型答案就变。

Response
Aggregate over many prompts; report consistency (ParaRel, BELIEF).多 prompt 聚合；报告一致性指标。

Takeaway 1

Same task, new tools. All four generations produce (x, relation, y) facts from text; what changes is how.

任务不变，工具在变。四代方法都是从文本中产出 (x, 关系, y) 事实，变的是做法。

Takeaway 2

Patterns never left. They became features and supervision in Gen 2, prompts and probes in Gen 3, and LLM-written rules in Gen 4.

模板从未离开：在第二代成为特征和监督信号，在第三代成为 prompt 和探测工具，在第四代成为大模型编写的规则。

Takeaway 3

Still open: specialized domains and leaf concepts, non-taxonomic relations, and evaluation that can be trusted.

尚未解决：专业领域和叶子概念、非分类关系，以及可信的评测。
