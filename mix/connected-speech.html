One idea behind every rule

一个原理，串起所有规则

Cheat sheets list these rules side by side. They are really one chain reaction, and it starts with stress.

速查表把这些规则并排摆放，其实它们是一条连锁反应，起点是重音。

  1. 1Stress重音

    A few syllables are louder, longer and higher.

    少数音节更响、更长、更高。

  2. 2Squeeze挤压

    Everything else is squeezed between those beats.

    其余音节被挤进节拍之间。

  3. 3Shrink弱化

    Squeezed vowels collapse into /ə/.

    被挤压的元音缩成 /ə/。

  4. 4Drop省略

    Some sounds disappear.

    有些音直接消失。

  5. 5Blend融合

    Word edges melt: sounds link and merge.

    词界消融：音和音连接、融合。

Learn in that order: stress first, then weak forms, then linking and blending.学习顺序：先重音，再弱读，最后连读与同化。本页章节就按这个顺序排列。

For listening为了听懂

Learn every rule here. You already know the words; you just don't recognise them inside the stream.

每一条都要熟。单词你都认识，只是进了语流就认不出来。

For speaking为了说

Get stress right first, especially the focus word, then weak forms. Don't force blends like “mee-chu”; they come with speed.

先做对重音，尤其是焦点词；其次是弱读。不要刻意模仿 “mee-chu” 这类同化，语速上来自然会有。

Research on intelligibility (Jenkins, 2000) found that the position of the main stress matters far more than blending.

01

Word stress

单词重音

Every word with two or more syllables gives one of them the spotlight. Put it in the wrong place and people may not recognise the word.

两个及以上音节的单词，总有一个音节最突出。重音放错，别人可能认不出这个词。

02

Rhythm and focus

节奏与焦点重音

In a sentence, stress lands on the words that carry meaning and arrives at a fairly steady beat. One of those stresses, the focus, is the strongest.

句子里，重音落在承载意思的词上，并按大致稳定的节拍出现。其中最强的那个叫焦点。

03

Weak forms

弱读

About forty small grammar words have two pronunciations: a strong one in the dictionary and a weak one you actually hear. The weak one almost always contains /ə/.

约四十个常用功能词有两种读法：词典里的强读式和对话里真正听到的弱读式。弱读式几乎都含 /ə/。

04

Dropped sounds

略读：省略与不完全爆破

At natural speed, some sounds disappear: a t or d trapped between consonants, the h of a small pronoun, even a whole syllable inside a word.

正常语速下，有些音会消失：夹在辅音之间的 t、d，小代词里的 h，甚至单词内部的整个音节。

05

Linking

连读与增音

Words don't stop at their edges. The end of one word hooks onto the start of the next, so a phrase sounds like one long word.

词与词之间不停顿。前一个词的结尾钩住后一个词的开头，整个短语听起来像一个长单词。

06

Blending

同化

When two sounds meet, they can merge into a new sound, or one can change to match the other. That is why “did you” sounds like “didja”.

两个音相遇，可能融合成新音，或一个音变形去迁就另一个。所以 “did you” 听起来像 “didja”。

07

The American flap T

美式闪音（常被误称为“浊化”）

In American English, the t in water, better and city is not a real t. It is a single quick tap of the tongue that sounds close to a light d.

美音里，water、better、city 中的 t 不是真正的 t，而是舌尖快速轻弹一下，听起来接近轻快的 d。

08

Intonation

语调

Pitch rises and falls across a sentence like a melody. The direction at the end tells listeners whether you have finished, whether you are asking, and how sure you are.

音高在句子中起伏，像一段旋律。句末的走向告诉听者：说完没有、是不是在问、有多确定。
