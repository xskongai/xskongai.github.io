Relation Extraction, Drawn Out关系抽取 · 图解知识体系（英文为主，中文辅助）

Relation extraction (RE) turns sentences into facts. It finds pairs of entities in text and names the relation between them, so that documents can become a knowledge graph. This guide maps the field on two axes, walks through seven generations of methods from Hearst's patterns (1992) to LLM prompting, and ends with a path for going deeper. 关系抽取把句子变成事实：找出文本中的实体对，并判断它们之间是什么关系，让文档变成知识图谱。本页按“抽什么 / 怎么抽”两条轴梳理整个领域，从 1992 年的 Hearst 模式讲到大模型提示，最后给出深入学习路线。

01

The core idea核心思想：NER 找“点”，RE 连“线”

Named entity recognition finds the dots. Relation extraction draws the lines between them. Each line is a triple (subject, relation, object). 命名实体识别（NER）负责找出“点”，关系抽取负责在点之间连“线”。每一条线就是一个三元组（主体，关系，客体）。

(Marie Curie, born_in, Warsaw)(Marie Curie, employee_of, University of Paris)
FORMALLY · 形式化定义 Given a text and two entity mentions e₁, e₂, predict r ∈ R ∪ {no_relation}. In the end-to-end version you get only the text and must output the whole set of triples. 给定文本和两个实体 e₁、e₂，预测关系 r（属于预定义集合 R，或“无关系”）。端到端版本只给文本，要输出全部三元组。

Where it is used: knowledge-base population (the TAC KBP “slot filling” task), search and question answering, biomedical literature mining (drug–drug and gene–disease interactions), and LLM pipelines that build a graph from documents before retrieval, as GraphRAG does. 应用：知识库补全（TAC KBP 槽填充）、搜索与问答、生物医学文献挖掘（药物相互作用、基因-疾病）、以及先从文档建图再检索的 LLM 流程（如 GraphRAG）。

02

The map: two independent axes知识地图：两条互相独立的轴

Every RE system answers two separate questions. What counts as a relation and where may its evidence sit? How does the system learn what a relation looks like? A paper's title usually names one point on each axis. “Pattern-based hypernym extraction” is Hearst's point. 每个关系抽取系统都要回答两个独立的问题：“抽什么”（什么算关系、证据在哪）和“怎么抽”（系统从哪学到关系长什么样）。论文标题通常各占一个点，Hearst 就是“基于模式的上下位关系抽取”。

03 · AXIS A

What to extract抽什么：三个刻度盘

Closed vs. open封闭关系 vs. 开放信息抽取

Closed RE fixes a schema first (ACE types, TACRED's 41 relations, Wikidata properties) and classifies into it. Open IE (TextRunner 2007, ReVerb 2011, OpenIE6 2020) keeps the relation as a text span. It never misses an unseen relation type, but you have to normalize “born in” and “a native of” yourself later. 封闭式先定好关系表再分类；开放式把关系直接保留为原文片段，覆盖面广，但“born in”和“a native of”这类同义说法要事后自己归一。

Sentence, document, cross-document粒度：句子级 / 文档级 / 跨文档

Sentence-level RE assumes both entities and the evidence sit in one sentence. Document-level RE drops that assumption: in DocRED (2019) at least 40.7% of the facts can only be extracted from multiple sentences, usually through coreference. Cross-document RE (CodRED, 2021) chains evidence across separate texts. 句子级假设证据都在一句话里；文档级不再假设——DocRED 中至少 40.7% 的事实需要跨句（通常靠共指）才能抽出；跨文档则要把多篇文本的证据串起来。

Classify a pair, or extract everything任务形式：关系分类 vs. 端到端三元组抽取

Relation classification gets the entity pair for free and only picks a label (SemEval-2010 Task 8, TACRED). End-to-end extraction must also find the entities. A pipeline runs NER and then RE; PURE (2021) showed that a carefully built pipeline is hard to beat. A joint model does both at once (Miwa & Bansal 2016; CasRel 2020; TPLinker 2020). Much of the push for joint models came from overlapping triples, which Zeng et al. (2018) sorted into three types: 关系分类：实体对已给定，只选标签；端到端：实体也要自己找。流水线先 NER 后 RE（PURE 2021 证明好的流水线很难被超越）；联合模型一次完成。推动联合模型的一大动因是“重叠三元组”。

Go deeper: beyond binary triples · 深入：超越二元三元组

Real facts often have more than two arguments or carry qualifiers. N-ary RE links three or more entities, as in drug–gene–mutation interactions (Peng et al., 2017). Hyper-relational facts add qualifiers: (Curie, award_received, Nobel Prize in Physics) plus point_in_time = 1903. Event extraction sits next door, with a trigger word and argument roles. 现实事实常常不止两个论元，或带限定条件：N 元关系（如药物-基因-突变）、超关系事实（三元组 + 时间等限定词）。事件抽取与之相邻（触发词 + 论元角色）。

THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题PURE beat joint models with a plain pipeline. What does that suggest about where the errors in end-to-end RE really come from?
Show a hint · 看提示Much of the gain came from good span representations and from feeding entity information to the relation model early. “Joint” is an architecture choice, not a guarantee of shared information. 很多收益来自好的片段表示、以及尽早把实体信息喂给关系模型；“联合”只是架构选择，不保证信息真正共享。
04 · AXIS B

How to extract: seven generations怎么抽：七代范式

The history of RE is a history of one question: where does the system's knowledge of a relation come from? Each generation moved part of that work from people to data, and then from data to models. 关系抽取的历史，本质上是在回答同一个问题：系统关于“某种关系长什么样”的知识从哪里来？每一代都把一部分工作从人转给数据，再从数据转给模型。

1992 →

Patterns and rules规则 / 模式匹配

source: hand-written rulesHearst lives here
Core idea
核心
Write lexico-syntactic templates that only match when the relation holds. Hearst (1992) found six that signal is-a (hyponymy) and harvested them from raw text with no labels. 手写“词汇-句法模板”，只在关系成立时匹配。Hearst 发现了六个表示“是一种”（上下位）的模板，无需任何标注即可从原始文本中收割。
Context
背景
Rule-based IE dominated the MUC evaluations (1987–1998); MUC-7 (1998) added a relation task with employee_of, product_of and location_of. MUC 评测时代以规则系统为主；MUC-7 首次设立关系任务（雇佣、产品、位置三种）。
Hearst patternExample fragment (Hearst 1992)Extracted
NP such as {NP,}* {or|and} NP“The bow lute, such as the Bambara ndang…”(Bambara ndang, is-a, bow lute)
such NP as {NP,}* {or|and} NP“…works by such authors as Herrick, Goldsmith, and Shakespeare”(Herrick, is-a, author)
NP {, NP}* {,} or other NP“Bruises, wounds, broken bones or other injuries…”(bruise, is-a, injury)
NP {, NP}* {,} and other NP“…temples, treasuries, and other important civic buildings”(temple, is-a, civic building)
NP {,} including {NP,}* {or|and} NP“All common-law countries, including Canada and England…”(Canada, is-a, common-law country)
NP {,} especially {NP,}* {or|and} NP“…most European countries, especially France, England, and Spain”(France, is-a, European country)

LIMIT · 瓶颈High precision, low recall. Each phrasing needs its own template, and every new relation needs a linguist. 精确率高、召回率低；每种说法都要一个模板，每个新关系都要语言学家手写。 NEXT · 下一步Let the machine write the patterns. 让机器自己学模板。
Go deeper: patterns never died · 深入：模式方法从未消亡

Roller, Kiela & Nickel (2018) compared Hearst patterns with distributional methods for hypernymy detection and found the patterns, smoothed with matrix factorization, still won. LLM prompts that say “X such as …” are Hearst patterns in a new place (see §07). Roller 等（2018）发现，经矩阵分解平滑后的 Hearst 模式在上下位关系检测上仍优于分布式方法。今天写 “X such as …” 的 LLM 提示，本质也是模板。

  1. Hearst, Automatic Acquisition of Hyponyms from Large Text Corpora COLING 1992
  2. Roller et al., Hearst Patterns Revisited ACL 2018
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题The demo's “false hit” fires on “Problems such as these”. Name two constraints that would block it without hurting recall much.
Show a hint · 看提示Require each hyponym to be a noun-phrase head (POS / chunk check, which rejects pronouns), and require it to occur under the same pattern in several sentences before accepting it (frequency threshold). 要求下位词是名词短语中心词（排除代词），并要求同一对在多句中重复出现才接受。
1998 →

Bootstrapping自举：种子滚雪球

source: a few seed pairs
Core idea
核心
Start from a handful of seed pairs. Find sentences that mention them, turn those contexts into patterns, use the patterns to find new pairs, and repeat. DIPRE (Brin, 1998) harvested book–author pairs from the web; Snowball (Agichtein & Gravano, 2000) added confidence scores for patterns and tuples. 从几个种子对出发：找到提到它们的句子 → 把上下文变成模板 → 用模板找新对 → 循环。DIPRE 在网上抽“书-作者”，Snowball 为模板和元组加了置信度。
CLASSIC EXAMPLE · 经典示例Snowball's seed set for organization → headquarters location was pairs like (Microsoft, Redmond), (IBM, Armonk), (Boeing, Seattle), run over a newswire corpus. Later systems pushed the idea further: KnowItAll (2005) and NELL (2010), a “never-ending” learner that kept reading the web. Snowball 用（微软，雷德蒙德）等组织-总部种子在新闻语料上运行；后来的 KnowItAll、NELL（永不停止的学习者）把这一思路做到网络规模。
LIMIT · 瓶颈Semantic drift, and no clean notion of “wrong”: the system never sees a negative example. 语义漂移；系统从没见过负例，不知道什么是“错”。 NEXT · 下一步Annotate data properly and train a classifier. 认真标注数据，训练分类器。
Go deeper · 深入
  1. Brin, Extracting Patterns and Relations from the World Wide Web (DIPRE) WebDB 1998
  2. Agichtein & Gravano, Snowball: Extracting Relations from Large Plain-Text Collections ACM DL 2000
  3. Carlson et al., Toward an Architecture for Never-Ending Language Learning (NELL) AAAI 2010
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题Bootstrapping is semi-supervised learning with a feedback loop. Which modern technique has the same failure mode?
Show a hint · 看提示Self-training and pseudo-labeling, including LLMs labeling data for their own fine-tuning: confirmation bias is semantic drift by another name. 自训练 / 伪标签（包括让 LLM 给自己造训练数据）：确认偏差就是语义漂移的另一个名字。
2003 →

Supervised: features and kernels有监督：特征工程与核方法

source: labels + hand features
Core idea
核心
Annotate entity pairs with relation labels. Describe each pair with features (words between the entities, entity types, parse paths) and train a classifier (Kambhatla 2004; Zhou et al. 2005), or skip explicit features and compare structures with a kernel (Zelenko et al. 2003; Culotta & Sorensen 2004; Bunescu & Mooney 2005). 给实体对标注关系标签；用特征（实体间的词、实体类型、句法路径）描述每一对，训练分类器；或者不显式写特征，用核函数直接比较结构。
Data
数据
The ACE program (2000–2008) supplied expert-annotated news; SemEval-2010 Task 8 gave a clean, directional benchmark. ACE 项目提供专家标注的新闻语料；SemEval-2010 Task 8 提供带方向的干净基准。
CLASSIC EXAMPLE · SemEval-2010 Task 8“The burst has been caused by water hammer pressure.” → Cause-Effect(e2, e1). Direction is part of the label: the pressure caused the burst, not the other way round. 9 directed relations + Other. 方向是标签的一部分：是压力导致爆裂，不是反过来。9 种有向关系 + Other。
LIMIT · 瓶颈Expensive expert labels, endless feature engineering, and parser errors flow straight into the classifier. 专家标注昂贵、特征工程无止境、句法分析错误直接传进分类器。 NEXT · 下一步Get labels for free from a knowledge base. 从知识库“免费”拿标签。
Go deeper · 深入
  1. Zelenko, Aone & Richardella, Kernel Methods for Relation Extraction JMLR 2003
  2. Kambhatla, Combining Lexical, Syntactic, and Semantic Features with Maximum Entropy Models ACL 2004
  3. Bunescu & Mooney, A Shortest Path Dependency Kernel for Relation Extraction EMNLP 2005
  4. Hendrickx et al., SemEval-2010 Task 8 SemEval 2010
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题A kernel needs no explicit feature vector. What does it still need from a human?
Show a hint · 看提示A choice of structure (tokens, constituency tree, dependency path) and a similarity over it. That is feature engineering moved one level up. 结构的选择和其上的相似度定义——特征工程只是上移了一层。
2009 →

Distant supervision远程监督：用知识库自动打标

source: a knowledge base
Core idea
核心
Mintz et al. (2009): if a knowledge base (Freebase) contains (e₁, r, e₂), assume every sentence that mentions both e₁ and e₂ expresses r. That gives free training labels at web scale, plus a lot of noise. 如果知识库里有（e₁，r，e₂），就假设所有同时提到 e₁ 和 e₂ 的句子都在表达 r。标签免费、规模巨大，但噪声也大。
Fixes
补救
Riedel et al. (2010) relaxed it to “at least one sentence is right” and built the NYT10 dataset, which turned the task into multi-instance learning. MultiR (2011) and MIML-RE (2012) allowed several relations per pair; PCNN (2015) and selective attention (Lin et al., 2016) learned which sentences to trust. Riedel 放宽为“至少一句是对的”（多示例学习），并发布 NYT10；MultiR、MIML-RE 允许一对实体有多个关系；PCNN 与选择性注意力学会“该信哪一句”。
LIMIT · 瓶颈Noise is structural: false positives from co-mentions, false negatives from an incomplete KB, and a held-out evaluation that is itself noisy. 噪声是结构性的：共现带来假阳性，知识库不全带来假阴性，连自动评测本身都有噪声。 NEXT · 下一步Better encoders: learn features with neural networks, and build larger clean datasets (TACRED, 2017). 用神经网络自动学特征，并构建更大的干净数据集。
Go deeper · 深入
  1. Mintz et al., Distant Supervision for Relation Extraction without Labeled Data ACL-IJCNLP 2009
  2. Riedel, Yao & McCallum, Modeling Relations and Their Mentions without Labeled Text ECML-PKDD 2010
  3. Zeng et al., Piecewise Convolutional Neural Networks (PCNN) EMNLP 2015
  4. Lin et al., Neural Relation Extraction with Selective Attention over Instances ACL 2016
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题Why does an incomplete knowledge base create false negatives, and why are those harder to fix than false positives?
Show a hint · 看提示A pair missing from the KB is labeled NA even when the text states the relation. Multi-instance learning only questions positive bags, so these silent errors are never revisited. 知识库缺失的实体对会被标为“无关系”；多示例学习只怀疑正例包，这些“沉默的错误”从不被复查。
2014 →

Neural networks神经网络：自动学特征

source: labels, auto features
Core idea
核心
Stop writing features. Feed word embeddings plus position embeddings (each token's distance to e₁ and to e₂) into a CNN (Zeng et al., 2014), an LSTM over the dependency path (Xu et al., 2015), an attention BiLSTM (Zhou et al., 2016), or a graph convolution over the pruned dependency tree (Zhang, Qi & Manning, 2018). 不再手写特征：把词向量 + 位置向量（每个词到两个实体的距离）送入 CNN、依存路径上的 LSTM、注意力 BiLSTM 或依存树上的图卷积。
Data
数据
TACRED (Zhang et al., 2017): 106,264 crowd-labeled examples from TAC KBP text, 41 relations plus no_relation. It became the standard sentence-level benchmark. TACRED：10.6 万条众包标注样本，41 种关系 + no_relation，成为句子级标准基准。
LIMIT · 瓶颈Representations are learned from a small labeled set, so every relation still needs thousands of examples. 表示只从少量标注数据学来，每种关系仍需成千上万样本。 NEXT · 下一步Pre-train on raw text first, fine-tune second. 先在海量原始文本上预训练，再微调。
Go deeper · 深入
  1. Zeng et al., Relation Classification via Convolutional Deep Neural Network COLING 2014
  2. Zhang et al., Position-aware Attention and Supervised Data Improve Slot Filling (TACRED) EMNLP 2017
  3. Zhang, Qi & Manning, Graph Convolution over Pruned Dependency Trees EMNLP 2018
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题The 2018 GCN still uses the dependency tree from era 3. Why would a neural model want a parser at all?
Show a hint · 看提示The tree is an inductive bias: it brings distant but related words together (the shortest-path idea). With little data the bias helps; pre-trained Transformers later learned much of that structure on their own. 依存树是一种归纳偏置，把远处相关的词拉近；数据少时有用，后来预训练模型自己学到了大部分结构。
2019 →

Pre-trained language models预训练模型 + 实体标记

source: pre-training + labels
Core idea
核心
Wrap the two entities in marker tokens, run a pre-trained Transformer, and classify from the hidden states at the markers. Matching the Blanks (Baldini Soares et al., 2019) set the recipe and also pre-trained relation representations by blanking out entities. Zhou & Chen's “improved baseline” (2022) showed that typed markers in plain punctuation, such as @ * person * Marie Curie @, are a strong default. 在两个实体两侧插入标记，过预训练 Transformer，取标记位置的隐状态做分类。MTB 确立了这一做法；Zhou & Chen 证明带类型的标点标记是很强的默认方案。
TYPED MARKERS · 带类型的标记（Zhou & Chen 2022） @ * person * Marie Curie @ was born in # ^ city ^ Warsaw # 用普通标点包住实体并写出实体类型，不必新增词表 token，效果反而更好。
LIMIT · 瓶颈The label set is frozen into the classifier head. A new relation means new labels and retraining. 标签集被固化在分类头里，新增关系就要重新标注、重新训练。 NEXT · 下一步Generate the structure as text and describe the schema in words. 把结构当文本生成，用自然语言描述关系模式。
Go deeper · 深入
  1. Baldini Soares et al., Matching the Blanks ACL 2019
  2. Zhou & Chen, An Improved Baseline for Sentence-level Relation Extraction AACL 2022
  3. Zhong & Chen, PURE: entity markers inside an end-to-end pipeline NAACL 2021
THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题Why does reading the [E1] start marker work better than averaging the entity's word pieces or using [CLS]?
Show a hint · 看提示The marker is a dedicated slot that attends to the whole sentence while “standing for” the entity, so it holds pair-specific context. [CLS] has to summarize the sentence for every possible pair. 标记是专门为该实体设的位置，同时关注全句；[CLS] 要为所有可能的实体对概括整句。
2021 →

Generative models and LLMs生成式与大模型

source: instructions, prompts
Core idea
核心
Treat extraction as text generation. Seq2seq models linearize triples (REBEL 2021, TANL 2021); UIE (2022) defined one structure language for many IE tasks; InstructUIE (2023) and GoLLIE (2024) instruction-tuned on IE datasets, GoLLIE with annotation guidelines written as code; and general LLMs are simply prompted with the schema. 把抽取当成生成：seq2seq 线性化三元组（REBEL、TANL）；UIE 用统一结构语言覆盖多种 IE 任务；InstructUIE、GoLLIE 在 IE 数据上做指令微调（GoLLIE 把标注指南写成代码）；通用 LLM 直接用提示描述关系模式。

Seq2seq linearization · REBEL

input:  Marie Curie was born in Warsaw.
output: <triplet> Marie Curie
        <subj> Warsaw
        <obj> place of birth
把三元组写成一串特殊标记文本，让模型直接“写”出来；关系名用自然语言（Wikidata 属性名）。

Prompting an LLM · 提示大模型

Relations: born_in, employee_of,
           spouse_of
Text: Marie Curie was born in Warsaw.
Return JSON triples.
→ [{"s":"Marie Curie","r":"born_in",
    "o":"Warsaw"}]
关系表写在提示里：换一套关系只需改提示，不必重新训练。
THE EVIDENCE IS MIXED · 证据并不一致 Han et al. (2023) evaluated ChatGPT on 17 datasets and 14 IE subtasks and found a large gap to state-of-the-art models, widest on the harder tasks; zero-shot relation classification on TACRED and triplet extraction were among the weakest results. Li, Wang & Ke (2023) reported that a summarize-then-ask prompt (SumAsk) made ChatGPT competitive with, and on TACRED above, the best supervised model. Prompt design and label-set size swing the results; TACRED's 42 labels are harder than SemEval's 10. Han 等发现零样本 ChatGPT 在关系分类和三元组抽取上偏弱；Li 等用“先总结再提问”的提示让 ChatGPT 在 TACRED 上超过最好的有监督模型。结论高度依赖提示设计和标签数量。
LIMIT · 瓶颈Hallucinated or off-schema triples, exact-match scoring that undercounts valid paraphrases, and cost at corpus scale. A common pattern: the LLM labels data, a small model does the extraction. 幻觉或越界三元组、精确匹配评测低估合理改写、全语料运行成本高。常见做法：LLM 造数据，小模型做抽取。 NOW · 现在See the open problems in §07. 见第 07 节开放问题。

THINK ABOUT IT · 思考题An LLM outputs (Marie Curie, birthplace, Warsaw) but the gold label is born_in. Is that an error? How would you score it fairly?
Show a hint · 看提示Constrain decoding to the schema, or map outputs to the label set (string normalization, embedding match, or an LLM judge) and report the mapping step separately, so its errors are visible. 约束解码到关系表，或把输出映射到标签集，并单独报告映射步骤的误差。
05 · A CROSS-CUTTING BRANCH

Few-shot and zero-shot RE少样本与零样本：横跨多代方法的支线

Few-shot RE asks a model to learn a new relation from a handful of examples. FewRel (Han et al., 2018; 100 relations, 70,000 instances from Wikipedia) framed it as N-way K-shot episodes, and prototypical networks became the classic baseline: average the K support examples into a prototype and assign the query to the nearest one. 少样本：每个新关系只给几个例子。FewRel 用“N 类 K 样本”的回合来评测；原型网络把 K 个样本平均成“原型”，查询句归到最近的原型。

Zero-shot RE goes further: no examples at all, only a description of the relation. The trick, again and again, is to rewrite the relation as another task the model already knows. 零样本：一个例子都没有，只有关系的描述。反复出现的技巧是：把关系改写成模型已经会做的另一种任务。

Scoring rules that trip people up

  • TACRED reports micro-F1 without no_relation; otherwise always predicting no_relation would look excellent. TACRED 的 micro-F1 不计 no_relation。
  • SemEval's official score is macro-F1 over the 9 relations, direction included. SemEval 官方：9 类宏平均 F1，计方向。
  • End-to-end: strict (boundaries + entity type + relation) and boundaries-only numbers are not comparable (Taillé et al., 2020). 端到端的严格评测与只看边界评测不可混比。

Label noise is real

Alt et al. (TACRED Revisited, ACL 2020) found many TACRED dev and test labels wrong; correcting them changed model rankings. Re-TACRED (Stoica et al., 2021) relabeled the whole set. TACRED 开发集和测试集有大量错标，纠正后模型排名会变；Re-TACRED 重新标注了全集。

07

Full circle, and the open problems兜了一圈，以及开放问题

1 · Faithful extraction

Can every generated triple be traced to an evidence span? Generative extractors can produce fluent triples the text never states. 可溯源：每个生成的三元组能否对应到原文证据？生成式模型会“编”出原文没说的三元组。

2 · Fair evaluation of generative RE

Exact match penalizes valid paraphrases; LLM judges bring their own bias. How should open outputs be scored? 评测：精确匹配惩罚合理改写，LLM 当裁判又有自身偏差。

3 · Document- and corpus-level reasoning

Coreference, multi-hop evidence and incomplete gold labels (DocRED → Re-DocRED) remain hard, even for long-context models. 文档级与语料级推理：共指、多跳证据、金标不全，长上下文模型也没解决。

4 · Schema following at scale

Real schemas have hundreds of relations with fine distinctions. Guidelines (GoLLIE) help; keeping a model inside the schema is still an open problem. 大规模关系模式：真实场景有成百上千个细粒度关系，让模型严格遵守模式仍未解决。

Two recent surveys for the current landscape: Ringwald et al. (2025) systematically review 2019–2024 work (34 surveys, 64 datasets, 104 models), and Diaz-Garcia & Diaz Lopez (2025) analyze 137 ACL papers from 2020–2023 and note that BERT-based models still dominate state-of-the-art results while LLMs show promise in few-shot settings. 两篇近期综述：Ringwald 等系统回顾 2019–2024（34 篇综述、64 个数据集、104 个模型）；Diaz-Garcia 等分析 2020–2023 年 137 篇 ACL 论文，指出 BERT 类模型仍主导 SOTA，LLM 在少样本场景中有潜力。

  4. LLM with a JSON schema. Score it by exact match and by manual check, and write down every case where exact match is unfair. 用 LLM + JSON 模式抽取，分别用精确匹配和人工检查打分，记录精确匹配不公平的案例。

Self-check · 自测

Q1Why does a Hearst matcher have high precision but low recall?
Answer · 答案Its templates are specific enough that a match is usually right, but most facts are written in other forms the templates never see. 模板足够具体，匹配到通常是对的；但大多数事实是用别的句式写的。
Q2State the distant-supervision assumption and its two failure modes.
Answer · 答案Every sentence mentioning a KB pair expresses the KB relation. It fails by false positives (co-mention without the relation) and false negatives (the relation is in the text but missing from the KB). 假阳性（共现但不表达该关系）与假阴性（文本表达了但知识库没有）。
Q3Why can't a single BIO tag sequence represent entity-pair overlap?
Answer · 答案Each token gets one tag, but in EPO the same two spans need two different relation labels at once. 每个词只能有一个标签，而 EPO 需要同一对实体同时带两个关系标签。
Q4In what sense is an LLM extraction prompt a Hearst pattern?
Answer · 答案Both encode human knowledge of how a relation is expressed as a text template; the prompt hands it to the model instead of a regex. 两者都把“关系如何表达”的人类知识写成文本模板，只是交给模型而不是正则。
