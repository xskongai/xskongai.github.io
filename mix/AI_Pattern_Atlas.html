WORKED EXAMPLE / 01

Bring me the mug from the kitchen.

机器人案例 · 把厨房里的马克杯拿给我

Follow the six decisions from a language instruction to a confirmed delivery.

Understand 理解目标

mug → IS_A → cup

Use the category hierarchy to look for cup candidates, then identify a mug.

先找杯具候选，再确认马克杯。

Locate 定位对象

ON(mug, counter)

Use the scene graph to narrow the search and camera observations to confirm the target.

用场景图缩小范围，再用相机确认目标。

Choose a skill 选择技能

USED_FOR(gripper, grasping)

Propose a grasp and check whether the gripper suits the object.

提出抓取动作，再检查夹具是否合适。

Plan 制定计划

REACHABLE(mug)

If the mug is out of reach, move the base before attempting a grasp.

够不到时先移动底盘。

Verify 执行与验证

HOLDING(robot, mug)

Check visual or gripper feedback. If the grasp failed, observe again and replan.

用反馈确认是否抓稳，失败就重新观测与规划。

Update 更新世界状态

Move(mug, counter, user area)

After delivery is confirmed, record the new location and time.

确认交付后更新位置与时间。

Text supplies general knowledge. Sensors establish current facts. A planner checks which actions are feasible.

文本给通用知识，传感器确认当前事实，规划器检查动作是否可行。SayCan 和 BEHAVIOR 提供了两种相关研究视角，见文末。
